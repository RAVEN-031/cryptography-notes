Poor Johan has been answering emails all day and many of the students are asking the same question. Can you read his messages?

# Encryption

Standard RSA encryption with small public exponent $e = 3$ and randomized $N$ on 7 messages.

# Solution

The challenge description says that multiple messages are the same and since hastad's broadcast attack requires at least 3 messages, there has to be a permutation of the sent messages which all are of the same plaintext.
